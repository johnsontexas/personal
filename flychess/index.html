Leaderboard — who beats the fly

Sign-in gives everyone a name here. Play a game to take a seat.
The fly scores every legal move by encoding the position it leads to onto 685 antennal-lobe projection neurons, letting the calyx sparsify that to 259 of 5,177 Kenyon cells, and reading the MBONs' vote. It plays the best-smelling move. Dopamine — reward prediction error — rewrites the KC→MBON synapses as you play, and a win floods it. Wiring, synapse counts and the neuron coordinates above are the FlyWire connectome. Elo is measured against the same built-in opponents the trainer uses, and is a ballpark.
